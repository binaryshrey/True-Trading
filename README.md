## True Trading

**True Trading** is an autonomous quantitative research and trading platform built to discover, evaluate, and evolve systematic trading strategies across **25+ cryptocurrency assets**. The system ingests high-frequency **1-minute market data** from sources including **CoinGecko and Yahoo Finance**, transforms market behavior into quantitative signals, and continuously backtests candidate strategies against historical price movements.

At the core of the platform is an **evolutionary strategy engine** that represents trading strategies as parameterized genomes and iteratively improves them through selection, crossover, and mutation. Strategies are evaluated using risk-adjusted performance metrics such as **Sharpe ratio, cumulative return, maximum drawdown, win rate, and portfolio fitness**, allowing stronger strategies to survive and evolve while weaker strategies are eliminated. The research pipeline is designed to test variations in momentum signals, moving-average windows, asset selection, position sizing, and risk tolerance while incorporating safeguards against overfitting.

True Trading connects the research environment to **Alpaca paper trading**, enabling promising strategies to move from historical simulation into market-facing experimentation without risking real capital. Alongside the backtesting engine, the platform combines real-time market data, portfolio analytics, macro and technical signals, and AI-assisted financial reasoning into a unified prediction and decision-support layer.

The system is designed as an end-to-end experimentation loop:

**Market Data → Signal Generation → Strategy Evolution → Backtesting → Risk Evaluation → Strategy Selection → Paper Trading → Feedback**

With an optimized analytics pipeline delivering **sub-200ms financial insights**, True Trading provides a 360° view of market conditions while serving as an experimental framework for researching how autonomous agents can discover and adapt systematic trading strategies over time.
